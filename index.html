About Us

The Great Sakya Monlam Foundation

The Great Sakya Monlam Foundation is dedicated to supporting Sakya Monlam activities and Buddhist initiatives centred on prayer, compassion, peace, harmony and the welfare of all sentient beings.

The Foundation brings together members of the Sangha, practitioners, devotees and Dharma friends for sacred prayer assemblies and related activities while supporting the preservation and continuation of the Sakya Buddhist tradition.

  • Purpose: facilitate prayer and Dharma activities.
  • Vision: cultivate peace, compassion and harmony.
  • Mission: preserve tradition and support beneficial Buddhist activities.
  • Community: welcome Sangha, practitioners and Dharma friends internationally.

35th Sakya Monlam for Universal Peace & Harmony

Venue: Mahabodhi Temple, under the sacred Bodhi Tree, Bodh Gaya, Bihar, India.
